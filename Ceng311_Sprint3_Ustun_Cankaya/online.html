Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do to make an appointment?

Fill out and submit the form on the online appointment page to make your appointment. You will be contacted before the appointment to inform you about the process. Additionally, you can reach us for any questions via the contact information available in the contact section.

How can I get information about session fees?

You can send an email or use the contact form to get information about session fees. You will receive a response as soon as possible.

Can I have a preliminary meeting before starting the therapy process?

Yes, a preliminary meeting can be arranged upon request. You can request a preliminary meeting to evaluate the process and get information about the therapy.

How can I join online therapy?

Online therapy sessions will be held via Google Meet. The connection details and necessary instructions will be sent to you before the session.

How long does a session last?

A therapy session typically lasts 45-50 minutes.

How soon will I receive a response if I send a message via email or contact form?

You will receive a response to your message within one day at the latest.
