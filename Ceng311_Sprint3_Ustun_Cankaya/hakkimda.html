Aybüke Üstün completed her undergraduate studies in Psychology at Ege University with high honors. In 2023, she began her Master's Program in Family Education and Counseling at Dokuz Eylül University. Her master's thesis was titled "Examination of the Relationship Between Attachment Styles and Mate Selection." As of February 2025, she successfully completed her master's degree and earned her expertise title.

During her undergraduate and graduate studies, Aybüke Üstün interned at Rehber Clinic. Since 2022, she has been providing online and face-to-face psychotherapy services in the fields of individual therapy and counseling. To make her specialization more qualified, she has completed training in Mindfulness-Based Therapy, Basic Sports Psychology, and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (Module 1). She further enhanced her skills by completing a 100-hour “Cognitive Behavioral Therapy Practitioner Training” and its supervision. "Change is possible for everyone and is the seed of every new beginning." With this philosophy, Aybüke Üstün aims to continually develop herself and is pleased to offer her services to you.
